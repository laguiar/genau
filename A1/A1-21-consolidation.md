# A1-21 — A1 consolidation: test and writing tasks

**Level:** A1 · **Time:** 2 sessions · **Prerequisites:** A1-01 … A1-20 · **Nicos Weg:** [Abschlusstest A1](https://learngerman.dw.com/de/abschlusstest-a1/l-43546310)
**Can-do:** I can pass an A1 grammar test at ≥ 80 % and write a form, a short e-mail and a message with the structures of A1.

## How to use this file

1. **Test** (30 min, no notes). Score in §3. Every wrong item → error log with its topic id → `review.py add-error`.
2. Below 80 %: go back to the two topics with most errors, redo their exercises, retest a week later.
3. **Writing** (2 × 15 min). Write by hand or in `writing/submissions/`, then have it corrected following [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) (say *Correct this*).
4. Then run the DW Abschlusstest A1 linked above as an external check.

## 2. Writing tasks

**W1 — Form (5 min).** Fill in as full words: Familienname · Vorname · Geburtsdatum (in words) · Adresse · Land · Muttersprache · Beruf · Telefonnummer (read as pairs, written in words).

**W2 — E-mail to a friend (15 min, ~50 words).** Your friend Anna invites you to a party on Saturday. Write back: (1) thank her and say you are coming, (2) ask what time it starts and where, (3) say what you will bring. Use: a greeting, *danken* + ■, one W-question, one separable verb, *bis Samstag!* and a closing.

**W3 — Message (10 min, ~40 words).** You cannot come to the German course tomorrow. Write to the teacher (*Sie*): why (*denn*), when you come back, and a request (*Können Sie mir bitte … schicken?*).

**Check before submitting:** every verb in position 2 / at the end; every noun capitalised; ß/ss; *Sie/Ihnen* capitals; *kein* vs *nicht*; ▲ *-en* on masculine objects; *mit dem / zum / zur*.
