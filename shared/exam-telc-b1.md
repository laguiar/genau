# telc Deutsch B1 (Zertifikat Deutsch) — format and tactics

The format and scoring below follow telc's published [exam format and Übungstest 1](https://www.telc.net/sprachpruefungen/deutsch/zertifikat-deutsch-telc-deutsch-b1/).

## Structure

| Part | Sub-part | Task | Items | Points | Time |
|------|----------|------|------:|-------:|-----:|
| **Leseverstehen** | Teil 1 | Match 5 headlines to 5 short texts (from 10) | 5 | 25 | **90 min** shared by Lesen and Sprachbausteine |
| | Teil 2 | Longer text (article), 5 multiple-choice statements (a/b/c) | 5 | 25 | |
| | Teil 3 | Match 10 situations to 12 adverts (some have no match: `X`) | 10 | 25 | |
| **Sprachbausteine** | Teil 1 | Letter with 10 gaps, 3 options each (grammar + connectors) | 10 | 15 | |
| | Teil 2 | Letter with 10 gaps, word bank of 15 (a–o) | 10 | 15 | |
| **Hörverstehen** | Teil 1–3 | Global, detail, selective listening; true/false | 20 | 75 | ~30 min |
| **Schriftlicher Ausdruck** | — | One informal or semi-formal e-mail addressing **4 content points** | 1 | 45 | 30 min |
| **Mündlicher Ausdruck** | Teil 1–3 | Contact, topic talk, joint task (pairs) | — | 75 | 15 min + 20 prep |

The published format has **no break during the written exam**. To pass, you need at least **135/225 written points**, including listening, and **45/75 oral points**.

## What grammar the exam really tests

**Sprachbausteine** items (both parts) draw again and again from the same pool:

1. Connectors and the word order they force: `weil / denn / deshalb`, `obwohl / trotzdem`, `wenn / als / ob / dass`, `damit / um … zu`, `sondern / aber`, two-part connectors.
2. Prepositions + case: `mit ■`, `für ▲`, `seit ■`, `wegen ◆/■`, two-way prepositions.
3. Verb forms: Perfekt participles, Präteritum of modals, Konjunktiv II (`hätte, würde, könnte`), Passiv (`wird … gemacht`), infinitive with `zu`.
4. Pronouns and articles: relative pronouns, `dieser/jeder/alle`, reflexives, possessives in the right case, `da-/wo-` compounds.
5. Adjective endings.
6. Fixed letter phrases: `Sehr geehrte`, `Mit freundlichen Grüßen`, `Ich würde mich freuen, wenn …`, `Vielen Dank im Voraus`.

Every one of these has a topic in [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md); B1-11 ties the connector system together and B1-22 is pure exam tactics.

## Schreiben — the 4-point letter

- Address all four **Leitpunkte** clearly and in a sensible order. A single short sentence can fulfil a point if it responds adequately and is understandable.
- Around **150 words** is a course practice target. Focus on answering the four points clearly.
- telc scores **Aufgabenbewältigung** (task fulfilment), **Kommunikative Gestaltung** (organisation, cohesion, register and e-mail conventions), and **Formale Richtigkeit** (grammar and spelling). Each criterion earns 5, 3, 1, or 0 points. Multiply the sum by 3 for a score out of 45.
- Match the register to the recipient and situation. Use `du` or `Sie` consistently.
- Protocol (30 min): 3 min read + underline the 4 points → 2 min plan one line per point → 20 min write → 5 min check: verb position, endings, capital letters, `Sie/Ihnen/Ihr` capitalised.

Templates and Redemittel: [writing-templates.md](writing-templates.md).

## Lesen tactics

- **Teil 1 (headlines):** read the 5 texts first, then find headlines; watch for distractors that repeat a word from the text but change the meaning.
- **Teil 2 (article):** read the statements first, then the text; answers appear in text order.
- **Teil 3 (adverts):** underline the *specific* requirement in each situation (day, price, place); the `X` (no match) usually applies to one or two situations.
- Time: aim for 45 minutes on Lesen so 20+ minutes remain for Sprachbausteine, where every item is a grammar decision you can reason through.
