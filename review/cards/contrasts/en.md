### A1-02-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] Why is *"Ich bin lernen Deutsch"* wrong for *"I am learning German"*?
A: German has no `-ing` continuous form; use Präsens: Ich lerne (gerade) Deutsch.
T: verbs contrast

### A1-04-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] English false friend: *wer* vs *wo*?
A: wer = who · wo = where (W-question word order: Wo wohnst du? Wer ist das?)
T: questions contrast

### A2-04-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] Translate *"I have lived here for 3 years"* (and name the tense trap).
A: Ich wohne seit drei Jahren hier. — German uses Präsens with seit, not Perfekt!
T: time prepositions contrast

### A2-07-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] English *"must not"* vs German *"müssen nicht"*?
A: English "must not" (prohibition) = nicht dürfen; German "müssen nicht" = don't have to.
T: modals contrast

### A2-15-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] Can you omit the relative pronoun as in English *"the man I saw yesterday"*?
A: Never in German: der Mann, den ich gestern gesehen habe.
T: relative contrast

### A2-16-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] False friend: what does German *"also"* mean, and how do you say English *"also"*?
A: German also = so / therefore (pos. 1 adverb); English "also" = auch / außerdem.
T: connectors contrast

### B1-04-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] English uses *"before / after"* before both nouns and clauses. How does German split them?
A: Before a noun: vor ■ / nach ■ (vor dem Essen, nach der Arbeit); before a clause (V-END): bevor / nachdem (bevor ich esse, nachdem ich gearbeitet hatte).
T: connectors prepositions contrast

### B1-06-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] English *"If I had time"* uses Simple Past. Why is *"Wenn ich Zeit hatte"* wrong for an unreal condition?
A: "Wenn ich Zeit hatte" means "Whenever I had time (in the past, real)". Unreal present needs Konjunktiv II: Wenn ich Zeit hätte.
T: konjunktiv contrast

### B1-07-en-01
Q: [🇬🇧] English *"The shop was closed at 6 pm"* uses *"was"* for both action and state. How does German distinguish them?
A: Action (someone locked it) = wurde geschlossen; State (it was shut) = war geschlossen.
T: passiv contrast
