You do **not** need Python or a terminal to use Genau flashcards in [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) (free on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android; paid on iOS). Just download the `.tsv` file(s) you want below and import them directly.

## How to import into Anki (3 steps)

1. **Download the file:** Click a file above, then click the **Download raw file** button (the downward arrow icon on GitHub).
2. **Open Anki** on your computer and click **File → Import…** (or press `Ctrl+Shift+I` / `Cmd+Shift+I`).
3. **Select the `.tsv` file** and click **Import**:
   - The files already include Anki header settings (`#separator:tab`, `#tags column:3`), so Anki automatically maps **Column 1 → Front**, **Column 2 → Back**, and **Column 3 → Tags**.
   - Every card is tagged by level and topic (e.g. `A1`, `A1-05`), so you can suspend future lessons and unsuspend each topic as you finish it.
   - Sync your Anki desktop with **AnkiWeb** to study your cards on your phone.
